# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SME founders and business owners running paid acquisition in Singapore (and broader APAC), spending S$5K–S$100K+/month on ads, frustrated that cheap leads aren't translating into profitable customers. Mostly visiting this lander after a referral or warm intro, on desktop during work hours or on mobile in the evening. The job to be done: decide in under 3 minutes whether Elevates can actually solve their CAC problem, then book a 45-minute Strategy Session.

## Product Purpose

A long-form direct-response landing page for **Elevates**, a performance-marketing agency. It re-frames the customer's pain ("my ad costs are exploding, my leads suck") from a lead-volume problem into a **Customer Acquisition Cost problem**, introduces the agency's proprietary "Profit Engine™" mechanism, and converts on an embedded Typeform strategy-session booking. Success = booked strategy calls from qualified, scale-ready businesses.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **operator, blunt, premium**. Voice is direct-response that knows the work — no fluff, no SaaS-cream "elevate your business" platitudes (despite the brand name). Tone shifts: editorial-italic for emotional punches ("Stop Paying More For Leads That *Don't Convert*"), monospace for proof points ("−42% CAC", "$220M+ revenue driven"). Visual: cinematic dark-studio cover, founder portraits, red brand accent on a near-black ground.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS-cream landing pages** (Stripe-adjacent muted-pastel + IBM Plex, Linear-adjacent off-white-with-purple-gradient). Elevates is not a SaaS tool; it's a high-touch service.
- **Generic "agency" sites** with abstract bezier shapes, gradient blobs, and "We Elevate Brands" headlines.
- **Editorial-magazine cosplay** (Fraunces / Newsreader display italic + drop caps + ruled separators). Wrong register for direct-response.
- **Cheap "agency landing" templates** (gradient-text headlines, 3-equal-cards "Our Services", stock handshake photos with blue overlay).

## Design Principles

1. **Show the receipts, not the promise.** Concrete numbers (`−42% CAC`, `+68% revenue per customer`, `$220M+`) beat adjectives. Mono numerals carry the proof points.
2. **One mechanism, named.** The Profit Engine™ is the visual + verbal centerpiece — animated, on a dedicated dark section, with the four pillars (Funnel / Offer / Creative / Backend) labelled.
3. **Editorial italic for the emotional beat, sans for the operational.** Playfair italic + brand red on punch words ("Don't Convert", "Lead Problem", "Scaling Your Profit"). Geist + Geist Mono everywhere else.
4. **The hero earns its weight.** A 4K scroll-scrubbed cinematic video of the two founders chatting → handshaking is the dominant visual moment. The rest of the page is calmer in service of it.
5. **The form is the action.** Embedded Typeform in the schedule section ends the page. No separate CTA button below it — the form IS the conversion surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Body text against light surfaces must hit ≥4.5:1; section eyebrows (uppercase mono labels) currently ride close to that floor and are an active audit concern. All hero motion (scroll-scrub + reveals + perpetual pulses + marquee) must respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Hero video must have a poster fallback for slow connections and reduced-motion users. Touch targets ≥44×44 on mobile (CTAs, form). Color is supportive, never sole carrier of meaning (e.g., red `−42%` is also accompanied by mono text and ▼ glyph).
